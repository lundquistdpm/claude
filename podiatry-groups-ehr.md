## Notable / Phreesia / similar
- On both runs, no podiatry group was confirmed using Notable, Phreesia, or Luma Health. The only Luma result was a generic orthopaedics marketing PDF.
- For the ModMed and athenahealth groups, intake, check-in, and eligibility checks appear to run on the EHR vendor's own patient engagement modules rather than on third-party platforms (inferred, not confirmed).
