## Observations
- **ModMed (EMA)** leads among the PE-backed roll-ups (Curalta, Balance Health/Weil). **athenaOne** is the other big platform (Upperline, StrideCare).
- **Notable / Phreesia:** still no podiatry group confirmed as a customer. Weave markets a podiatry product but names no group. Patient engagement at these groups seems to come mostly from the EHR vendor's own module (ModMed).
